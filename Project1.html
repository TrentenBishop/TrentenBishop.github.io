Project 1: Crime Rate Analysis

In this project, I analyzed the 2023 crime report to identify trends and patterns within the data. I started by reviewing the dataset, which included various crime categories and their respective statistics across different regions.

My primary goal was to uncover key insights related to crime rates and identify areas that experienced significant changes or patterns in criminal activity. Through this process, I applied data analysis techniques such as:

  • Data cleaning and preprocessing to handle missing or inconsistent data.
  • Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to visualize crime trends over time and across different locations.
  • Correlation analysis to identify any relationships between various crime types and socio-economic factors.
  • Building data models to predict potential future crime hotspots based on historical data.

Ultimately, the project helped provide a deeper understanding of crime trends in 2023, which could be used to inform public policy or law enforcement strategies. The insights gained from this analysis highlight the importance of data-driven decision-making in addressing societal issues.

I applied various programming and analytical tools such as Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib to carry out this analysis, and I’m excited to share the results in my portfolio.
